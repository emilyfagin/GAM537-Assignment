
## **Exiled Prototype**

Our prototype presents a fully explorable village environment designed to showcase environmental storytelling through detailed landscape composition, lighting, and interactivity. Built in Unreal Engine 5, it demonstrates both technical and artistic integration across level design, materials, and Blueprint systems while remaining faithful to the game’s concept: an immersive, narrative-driven trading simulation experience set in a quiet, mysterious rural settlement.

### Functional Landscape and Environment Design

The village terrain was sculpted using Unreal Engine Landscape tools and hand picked assets, emphasizing casual high-fantasy tropes. Pathways, elevation changes, and clustering of buildings guide the player organically toward key areas of interest, supporting intuitive exploration and an intuitive understanding of resource procurement. 

### Foliage, Materials, and Lighting Integration

Hand painted foliage layers populate grass, shrubs, trees and mushrooms using UE5’s foliage system. Supporting the exaggerated high-fantasy tropes and nature elements while also providing room for improvement for future features like wildlife.

### Particle Effects and Atmospheric Polish

 Ambient particles like flames, wind, drifting leaves and chimney smoke build upon the village aesthetics. These VFX react to camera exposure in order to provide a more optimized performance. 

### Interactive Blueprint Systems

An interactive campfire was implemented to demonstrate gameplay functionality. The Blueprint handles player proximity and smooth animation playback. Its modular design allows expansion into future interactables such as levers or switches or even NPC interactions.

### Switchable Camera Perspectives

The prototype supports both first-person and third-person perspectives through a custom UI. This is meant to showcase future intent for future UI improvements and the projects focus on immersion without losing quality of life features.

### Narrative and Environmental Storytelling

Story elements are conveyed through spatial arrangement and environmental cues. Areas of interest where future guild leaders which the player will seek to gain favor from will be located around areas which make the most sense contextually. These areas currently are : open market, small farm and the cave. Future versions will implement a witches hut for the guild of magic, a hunter's hut for the guild of rangers, a blacksmith's forge for the guild of blacksmiths, a fisherman's hut for the fishing guild and village checkpoints, located on both ends of the map, ran by the knight's guild.
